# YOKE Token — Whitepaper Outline
**Draft v0.1 — Not for public offering**

## 1. Overview
YOKE is developing a utility-token concept connected initially to YOKE Stock Analysis Bot. The first intended use is redemption of YOKE Token for eligible premium subscription access.

## 2. Problem
Users need a clear, reliable way to access premium stock-analysis tools. The product must demonstrate actual recurring utility before a token distribution is justified.

## 3. Product
YOKE Stock Analysis Bot provides analysis of Indonesian stock-market data based on end-of-day information. It is not a live-market execution or brokerage service. Product descriptions, data sources, update schedules, and limitations must be kept accurate.

## 4. Token utility
- Subscription redemption as the first utility.
- Optional loyalty discounts only after usage and economic testing.
- No staking yield, revenue share, dividend, equity, or guaranteed return at launch.

## 5. Token design
Insert final chain, contract, supply, allocation, vesting, and token-handling design only after technical and legal review.

## 6. Redemption mechanism
Explain wallet proof, quote calculation, on-chain transaction, backend verification, premium activation, refund handling, and support.

## 7. Distribution
Describe testnet, closed beta, and broader distribution gates. Do not imply that deployment guarantees exchange listing or liquidity.

## 8. Governance and treasury
Describe multisignature custody, signers, spending policy, vesting wallets, disclosure cadence, and incident response. Do not claim decentralized governance unless it exists in practice.

## 9. Risks
Include market volatility, liquidity, smart-contract, wallet, custody, oracle/pricing, operational, product, regulatory, tax, and jurisdictional risks.

## 10. Legal and compliance
Identify the issuer, applicable jurisdictions, distribution restrictions, consumer disclosures, AML/CFT considerations where applicable, tax analysis, and professional legal opinions. A global target does not remove local legal obligations.

## 11. Disclosures
State clearly that YOKE Token is not equity, debt, a deposit, a security entitlement, or a guaranteed investment return unless a competent legal analysis determines otherwise. Do not make unsupported legal classification claims.
