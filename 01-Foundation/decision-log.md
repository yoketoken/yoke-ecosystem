# YOKE Decision Log

**Version:** 2.0
**Last Updated:** 20 November 2026

This document records the major strategic and technical decisions for the YOKE ecosystem.

## 1. Project Identity

| Item | Decision |
|---|---|
| Project Name | YOKE |
| Tagline | Connect. Empower. Automate. |
| Business Direction | Digital technology and blockchain utility |
| Target Market | Global |
| Documentation | English and Bahasa Indonesia |

## 2. Initial Ecosystem Scope

**Decision:** Focus on one initial product supported by YOKE Token.

| Component | Role |
|---|---|
| YOKE Token | Proposed utility token |
| YOKE Stock Analysis Bot | Sole initial product |

Other proposed business pillars, including Automation and Digital Services as separate product lines, are outside the initial development scope.

## 3. Token Utility

**Decision:** Premium subscription redemption.

Users may redeem YOKE Token to pay for eligible premium subscription packages of YOKE Stock Analysis Bot.

Initial design:
- Users do not need to retain tokens after successful redemption.
- Wallet ownership is verified through a signed message.
- Actual on-chain payment must be verified before premium activation.
- Additional loyalty discounts are deferred until the redemption model is validated.

## 4. Distribution Strategy

**Decision:** Gradual, milestone-based distribution.

Development sequence:
1. Research and design.
2. Testnet prototype.
3. Security and legal review.
4. Closed utility beta.
5. Broader distribution, subject to approval.

No public token sale or mainnet launch is authorized by this decision log.

## 7. Technical Principles

- Use established smart-contract standards.
- Prefer fixed supply without post-launch minting.
- Avoid transfer taxes in the initial design.
- Never request user seed phrases or private keys.
- Use signed-message wallet verification.
- Prevent duplicate transaction processing.
- Test on a testnet before mainnet consideration.
- Obtain independent security review before real-token launch.

## 8. Financial and Legal Principles

- No guaranteed investment returns.
- No guaranteed exchange listing or liquidity.
- No promised dividends, interest, or staking yield.
- No claim of company ownership through the token.
- Distribution and redemption must undergo applicable legal review.

## 9. Open Decisions

The following remain unresolved:
- Final blockchain network.
- Legal issuer and jurisdiction.
- Subscription conversion-price methodology.
- Token burn or redemption-vault mechanism.
- Final vesting and treasury custody.
- Security audit arrangements.
- Jurisdiction-specific distribution requirements.

All proposed decisions remain subject to documented revision and approval.
