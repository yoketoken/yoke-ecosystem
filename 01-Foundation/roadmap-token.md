# YOKE Token Roadmap
**Draft v0.1 — 20 November 2026**

## Phase 0 — Design and feasibility
- Freeze the initial utility scope around YOKE Stock Analysis Bot.
- Document subscription packages, cost structure, and expected redemption economics.
- Obtain legal advice on issuer structure, token classification, distribution, and payment-related restrictions.
- Compare candidate networks and custody models.
- Do not sell or publicly distribute tokens.

**Gate:** legal and economic feasibility documented.

## Phase 1 — Prototype and testnet
- Implement wallet connection and signed-nonce verification.
- Build redemption quote and transaction monitoring.
- Implement transaction deduplication, subscription activation, refunds, and reconciliation.
- Deploy only to a testnet.
- Run unit, integration, and failure-mode tests.

**Gate:** end-to-end testnet redemption works reliably; no real funds accepted.

## Phase 2 — Closed utility beta
- Proceed only after legal review and security review.
- Invite a limited group under clear terms.
- Measure redemption success, support load, retention, and product value.
- Publish token allocation wallets and vesting information if public distribution is approved.

**Gate:** product utility and operations are proven.

## Phase 3 — Broader ecosystem distribution
- Consider broader distribution only after all legal, security, operational, and consumer-protection gates.
- Publish final whitepaper and risk disclosures.
- Do not promise exchange listing, liquidity, or token-price performance.

## Non-goals for the initial phase
- No CEX listing promises.
- No yield or staking rewards.
- No speculative marketing or price targets.
- No public mainnet launch before legal and security approval.
