# YOKE — Roadmap | Peta Jalan

**Version 1.0-draft | Planning horizon: 12 months**

This roadmap is milestone-based, not a promise of delivery dates. Progress depends on available time, validation, security, and compliance.

Roadmap ini berbasis pencapaian, bukan janji tanggal peluncuran. Kemajuan bergantung pada waktu, validasi, keamanan, dan kepatuhan.

## Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–2)

**Objectives**
- Approve vision, mission, and brand positioning.
- Define business pillars and initial customer segments.
- Create GitHub repository and documentation structure.
- Record assumptions, risks, and open questions.

**Deliverables**
- README.
- Vision & Mission.
- Business Model.
- Roadmap.
- Initial risk register.

**Exit criteria:** documents reviewed and version-controlled.

## Phase 2 — Product validation (Weeks 3–6)

**Business track**
- Select one initial customer problem.
- Interview potential users.
- Build a small demo or minimum viable product.
- Test willingness to use and pay.

**Technology track**
- Set up development repository and coding standards.
- Research BNB Chain and alternative infrastructure.
- Build a local/testnet-only ERC-20-compatible prototype if a token use case remains justified.
- Write tests for supply, allocation, and transfers.

**Exit criteria:** evidence of user need and a working prototype; no public token sale.

## Phase 3 — MVP and operating foundation (Months 2–3)

- Release a limited MVP.
- Publish product documentation and limitations.
- Establish support and incident procedures.
- Track activation, retention, and product quality.
- Review data licensing, privacy, and regulatory boundaries.

**Exit criteria:** real users can complete the intended task and feedback is recorded.

## Phase 4 — Ecosystem integration (Months 4–6)

- Improve the first product based on evidence.
- Explore integration between Financial and Automation products.
- Establish partner and community guidelines.
- Assess whether blockchain adds measurable value.
- Commission independent security and legal reviews if token work advances.

**Exit criteria:** a defensible utility case and documented risk assessment.

## Phase 5 — Controlled expansion (Months 7–9)

- Expand customer acquisition in Indonesia.
- Prepare localization and support for selected global markets.
- Improve billing, operations, monitoring, and security.
- Publish transparent business and product updates.

**Exit criteria:** stable operations and repeatable customer acquisition.

## Phase 6 — Strategic review (Months 10–12)

- Review product-market fit and revenue.
- Review security, legal, and regulatory readiness.
- Decide whether to continue, pause, or abandon token issuance.
- If justified, prepare a separate token-launch proposal and approval process.

**Exit criteria:** documented go/no-go decision. Mainnet launch is not automatic.

## Parallel workstreams | Jalur kerja paralel

| Business track | Technology track |
|---|---|
| Customer discovery | Architecture research |
| MVP and services | Prototype and tests |
| User feedback | Security engineering |
| Pricing and revenue | Infrastructure |
| Partnerships | Integration |

## Go / no-go gates

A public token launch must be paused if any of the following is missing:
- Demonstrated utility and a real user need.
- Legal/regulatory assessment for target jurisdictions.
- Independent smart-contract security review appropriate to the risk.
- Transparent distribution, vesting, and treasury controls.
- Clear user disclosures and operational support.
- Adequate budget for deployment, monitoring, and incident response.
