# YOKE Token — Decision Log Addendum
**Date:** 20 November 2026

## Confirmed strategic decisions
1. YOKE Token is the current development focus.
2. YOKE Stock Analysis Bot is the single initial product supporting token utility.
3. Target market ambition: global.
4. Distribution approach: gradual and milestone-based.
5. Primary utility: premium subscription access through token redemption.
6. User does not need to keep tokens in the wallet after a successful redemption.

## Recommended design decisions
1. Start with subscription redemption; defer balance-based loyalty tiers.
2. Do not launch staking, yield, dividends, revenue sharing, or governance claims at the initial stage.
3. Use wallet signature verification to prove wallet control; never request private keys or seed phrases.
4. Verify actual on-chain payment and transaction details before activating premium access.
5. Use testnet and test tokens before any mainnet launch.
6. Treat BNB Smart Chain as a candidate, not a final network decision.
7. Keep the tokenomics supply and allocations as proposals pending legal, economic, and technical review.

## Still open
- Legal issuer and jurisdiction.
- Network and contract architecture.
- Token price discovery / subscription conversion method.
- Redemption burn or escrow model.
- Final vesting and custody.
- Legal review in Indonesia and target countries.
- Independent security review and audit.
